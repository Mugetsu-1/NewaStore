Orders & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept eSewa, Khalti, Credit/Debit cards (Stripe), PayPal, Bank Transfer and Cash on Delivery.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All transactions are encrypted and processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways. We never store your card details.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel pending, confirmed or processing orders from your account. Once shipped, orders can't be cancelled but may be returned under our return policy.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed within 24 hours and delivered in 2–5 business days across Nepal, depending on your location.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! Free standard shipping is available on orders over {{ currency_symbol }} {{ site_settings.free_shipping_threshold|default:5000|floatformat:0 }}.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number and can follow its progress from your account.

Returns & Warranty

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy on most physical products. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Digital products are non-refundable once delivered.

Do products come with a warranty?

All physical products include the manufacturer's warranty. Contact our support team for warranty claims.
