Orders & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept eSewa and Nay Bank Transfer.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All transactions are encrypted and processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways. We never store your card details.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel pending, confirmed or processing orders from your account. Once your game keys have been issued and emailed, the order is fulfilled and can't be cancelled — see our Refund Policy for eligible cases.

Delivery

How do I receive my game?

Every product is a digital PC game key. As soon as your payment is confirmed, your key is emailed to you and shown on your order page — there's nothing to ship.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is instant for confirmed payments. If you pay by bank transfer, your key is released once we verify the deposit.

Is there any delivery fee?

No. Digital delivery by email is always free.

Refunds

What is your refund policy?

Because keys are delivered digitally, an order is non-refundable once the key has been revealed or redeemed. If a key is invalid or fails to activate, contact us and we'll replace it or issue a refund. See our Refund Policy for full details.

What if my key doesn't work?

Reach out to our support team with your order number. We'll verify the key and, if there's a problem on our side, send a working replacement or refund you.
