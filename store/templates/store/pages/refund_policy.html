Refund Policy

How refunds work for digital game keys delivered by email.

Digital Delivery

Every product on {{ site_settings.site_name|default:'NewaStore' }} is a digital PC game key. The moment your payment is confirmed, your key is emailed to the address on your order and shown on your order page — there is nothing to ship and no delivery fee.

Refunds

When you're eligible

Because keys are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, an order becomes non-refundable once the key has been revealed or redeemed. You may request a refund if:

  • The key is invalid, duplicate, or fails to activate
  • The key does not match the product you purchased
  • You were charged more than once for the same order

How to request a refund

  1. Contact our support team with your order number and a description of the issue
  2. Do not redeem the key if you believe there is a problem — a redeemed key cannot be refunded
  3. We'll verify the key and, where the fault is on our side, send a working replacement or issue a refund
  4. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5–10 business days

Non-refundable

Keys that have been successfully revealed or redeemed, and orders flagged as fraudulent, are not eligible for a refund.
